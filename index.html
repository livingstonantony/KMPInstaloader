Privacy Policy

KMPInstaloader · Effective Date: October 7, 2026

KMPInstaloader ("the App") is an open-source application developed by Livingston Antony. The App allows users to retrieve and download publicly available media from Instagram posts.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how KMPInstaloader handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

KMPInstaloader does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

The App may process the following information when you use its features.

Instagram URLs

When you provide an Instagram post URL to the App, the URL is processed to identify the corresponding public Instagram post.

The App uses the provided URL to retrieve publicly available information associated with that post, such as media content and publicly available post metadata.

Downloaded Media

When you choose to download media, the resulting files may be stored locally on your device according to the permissions and storage mechanisms provided by your operating system.

KMPInstaloader does not intentionally upload your downloaded media to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by KMPInstaloader is used only to provide the functionality of the App, including:

  • Processing Instagram post URLs.
  • Retrieving publicly available Instagram post information.
  • Displaying publicly available media.
  • Downloading media selected by the user.
  • Saving downloaded media to the user's device.

We do not use the information processed by the App for advertising profiling, selling personal information, or creating user profiles.

3. Data Sharing

KMPInstaloader does not sell, rent, or intentionally share users' personal information with third parties.

The App communicates with Instagram-related public endpoints to retrieve publicly available content requested by the user.

The handling of information by third-party services, including Instagram, is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

4. Third-Party Services

KMPInstaloader may interact with third-party services, including Instagram, when retrieving publicly available content.

KMPInstaloader does not require users to provide their Instagram username or password and does not request Instagram login credentials.

Third-party services may independently collect or process information according to their own policies. Users should review the privacy policies and terms of those services for more information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

KMPInstaloader does not maintain a user account or a centralized database containing users' personal information.

Information processed during the use of the App is not intentionally retained by the developer after it is no longer required to provide the requested functionality.

Downloaded media files are stored locally on the user's device. Users can delete downloaded files using the device's file-management features or the App's available deletion functionality, if provided.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection and retention of user information.

Network communication used by the App is performed using standard network protocols. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

KMPInstaloader is not specifically directed toward children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. User Responsibility

KMPInstaloader is intended to provide access to publicly available content.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of downloaded content complies with applicable laws, copyright requirements, and the terms and policies of the relevant platforms.

KMPInstaloader does not encourage unauthorized access to private accounts or private content.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable requirements, or our data-handling practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
